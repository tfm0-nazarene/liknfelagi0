Progression & Fatigue Management Rules

Principle

Implementation Protocol

 

Double Progression System

Begin at the bottom rep target (e.g., 6 reps) at RIR 2. Maintain that load until you hit the top rep target (e.g., 8 reps) across all sets with pristine technique. Then increase backpack weight or advance to a harder lever variation.

Leverage Progressions

Advance push-ups from Standard → Feet-Elevated → Backpack Weighted → Archer Push-Ups. Advance pike push-ups from Floor → Elevated Feet → Wall Handstand Push-Ups.

Scheduled Deload (Week 5 or 6)

Reduce total sets per exercise by 50% (e.g., from 4 sets to 2 sets) and reduce backpack load by ~30%. Promotes connective tissue, tendon, and CNS recovery.

NEAT & Step Goal

Aim for 5,000–8,000 daily steps through walking as the primary physical activity anchor for sustainable fat loss without joint fatigue.
